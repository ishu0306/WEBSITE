About harry potter

Harry Potter is a famous fantasy book series written by J. K. Rowling. The series follows the adventures of a young wizard named Harry Potter and his friends, Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Throughout the seven books, Harry learns magic, faces many challenges, and fights against the dark wizard Lord Voldemort. The Harry Potter series teaches important values such as friendship, courage, honesty, and perseverance. It has become one of the most popular book series in the world and has also been adapted into successful movies enjoyed by millions of fans.

THE FIRST PART(Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone)

**Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone** is the first book in the Harry Potter series, written by J. K. Rowling. The story introduces Harry Potter, an eleven-year-old boy who discovers that he is a wizard. He receives a letter inviting him to study at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. At Hogwarts, Harry makes close friends, learns magic, and discovers the truth about his past. He also learns that the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, who killed his parents, is still a threat. The book follows Harry's adventures as he and his friends try to protect the mysterious Philosopher's Stone, a magical object that can grant immortality. The story is full of friendship, bravery, mystery, and magic.

THE SECOND PART(Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets)

**Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets** is the second book in the Harry Potter series, written by J. K. Rowling. In this story, Harry returns to Hogwarts for his second year. Strange events begin to occur when students are mysteriously attacked and a hidden chamber called the Chamber of Secrets is said to have been opened. Harry, Ron, and Hermione investigate the mystery and discover that a dangerous creature is hiding within the school. With courage and determination, Harry faces many challenges and eventually defeats the monster, saving Hogwarts and its students. The book highlights the values of bravery, friendship, and loyalty.

THE THIRD PART(Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban)

**Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban** is the third book in the Harry Potter series, written by J. K. Rowling. In this story, Harry begins his third year at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. He learns that a dangerous prisoner named Sirius Black has escaped from Azkaban prison and is believed to be searching for him. As the story unfolds, Harry discovers surprising truths about his parents, Sirius Black, and his own past. With the help of his friends Ron and Hermione, Harry uncovers the real mystery and learns that Sirius is not the villain everyone believes him to be. The book is filled with adventure, suspense, friendship, and important lessons about trust and loyalty.
